Run on iPhone & Android

Install as a full-screen offline phone app

🍎 Apple iOS (iPhone / iPad)
  1. Open this game in Safari on your iPhone.
  2. Tap the Share button ↑ at the bottom of the screen.
  3. Select Add to Home Screen. It launches full-screen like a native app!
🤖 Android (Chrome / Edge)
  1. Open this game in Chrome on Android.
  2. Tap the ⋮ Menu in the top-right corner.
  3. Tap Add to Home screen or Install app.
💾 How On-Device High Scores Work

Your high scores and lifetime stats are stored directly on your phone using dual localStorage + IndexedDB persistence. They stay saved even when you close the app or play offline in Airplane Mode.
